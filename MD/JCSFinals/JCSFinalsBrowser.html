JCS Finals Collection

This site brings together written final exam questions from four Junior Clerkship cohorts: 2017, 2018, 2019, and 2020, organized into a single searchable question bank purely for study and revision purposes.

The original exam files are publicly available in this Google Drive folder, if you'd like to cross-check anything against the source.

A number of questions, in the original source documents, were incomplete, poorly formatted, or missing options in the original documents. These were reconstructed to read as complete questions while keeping the original intent, use "Show Source" to compare questions with the original extracted text where available.

In MCQs with incomplete choices, new options may have been added to complete a full set of 5 options. You can see the original options by showing the source.

Answers and notes from the original sources were intentionally left out.
